# ChaiCart refund policy (fictional)

1. Refunds are available within 7 days of delivery for damaged, wrong or missing items.
2. Refunds of Rs 1,000 or less can be approved by the support assistant.
3. Refunds above Rs 1,000 must be approved by a manager before money moves.
4. Opened food items are not refundable unless they arrived damaged or spoiled.
5. If an order is late by more than 5 days, offer free delivery on the next order.
6. Never promise a refund before the order has been checked.
